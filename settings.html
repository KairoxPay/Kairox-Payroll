Statutory rates

Employee-side rates used to calculate every payroll and payslip.

Check these against taj.gov.jm, nis.gov.jm and nht.gov.jm each April. A change applies to new and unsaved payrolls only. Payrolls already processed keep the rates they were run with, and every change is recorded in the audit trail.

Taxable income above the threshold, up to this amount, is taxed at the lower rate.

Users and roles

Admins approve and process payroll. Payroll clerks prepare it. HR officers look after people and leave without seeing pay. Managers approve their own team's leave. Viewers can only read.

To add someone, first create their login in Supabase (Authentication, then Users), then give them a role here. Someone with no role sees nothing. Employees do not need a role here. People who only need their own payslips and leave get access on the People page (Give access), which opens My portal and nothing else. A Viewer can see everyone's pay.
