How to pay: click Download file, check your NCB Originator ID and company account, and download the NCB ACH file (.txt). In NCB Business Online Banking, upload it as an ACH file, check the total and approve it. The file pays every bank in Jamaica that is on the ACH list. Kairox Pay does not move any money, and a CSV summary is there for your records.
