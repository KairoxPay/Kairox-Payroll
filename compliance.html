Annual filings

The employer's annual return (S02), due by 31 March, and the P24 certificate each employee must receive at the end of the year, for each year you processed payroll.

Loading…

P24: TAJ's Employers' Guide says each employee must be given a P24, a certificate of gross emoluments and tax deducted, "at the end of each year". It gives no fixed date, so the date shown is only our target (31 January), so employees can file their own returns by 15 March. P45 (for someone who leaves part-way through the year) is prepared from the Leavers page. S02 is filed online by 31 March; the guide (2019) lists a $5,000 penalty for each month it is late. Employers must keep payroll records for at least seven years. Source: TAJ Employers' Guide. Always confirm with TAJ.
