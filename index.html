Limited Company tax rate

A Limited Company doesn't pay NIS/NHT/Education Tax on its own profit — those only apply to individuals. If the company has employees, their statutory deductions are handled in the Payroll tab; this section is only the company's own corporate income tax (Form IT02).

What do you need reminders for?

Deadlines shown are the standard published due dates. If a date falls on a weekend or public holiday, TAJ typically moves it to the next business day — always confirm the exact date on taj.gov.jm close to the deadline, since dates are occasionally adjusted by official notice (as happened with the 2025 corporate return deadline).
