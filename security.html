Account security

Protect your Kairox Pay sign-in with a code from your phone, as well as your password.

?

Two-step sign-in

After your password, Kairox Pay asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or similar). Someone who learns your password still cannot get in.

Who has two-step sign-in

Everyone with a role in this company. Admins, managing directors and payroll officers should all have it on.
