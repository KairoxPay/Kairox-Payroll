Leavers

Record when someone's employment ends. They stay on the payroll, so their final pay can be processed, until an admin marks them as left. Then prepare their P45.

EmployeeLast dayReasonPayP45
Loading…

How it works: 1. Record the departure. 2. Check their Final pay (vacation pay and the last month). 3. Run and process their final payroll. 4. An admin clicks Mark as left. 5. Open the P45 figures, prepare the form (three copies: one for Tax Administration Jamaica and two for the employee) and record it as given. The payroll will not approve a pay period that starts after someone's last day.
